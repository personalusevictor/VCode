Interface icons: Lucide (ISC), https://lucide.dev/ and https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide .
Technology symbols: Devicon (MIT), https://github.com/devicons/devicon .
HTML5 logo: W3C, https://www.w3.org/html/logo/ , CC BY 3.0.
CSS3 shield: Devicon (MIT), https://github.com/devicons/devicon/blob/master/icons/css3/css3-original.svg .
