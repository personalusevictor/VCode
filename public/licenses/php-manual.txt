VCode extracts summaries, signatures, parameters, return values, notes and
examples from the DocBook sources and rearranges them into reference cards.
The catalog includes class and interface declarations, methods, constants and
predefined variables. Cards link to their corresponding manual page. Original VCode explanations
and runnable samples are maintained in src/docs/data/php-explanations.json.
